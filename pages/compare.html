Compare approaches

These are evaluation questions, not a claim that one approach is universally better. Requirements, operating context, and implementation determine tradeoffs.

FrameIQ concepts and generic CCTV analytics

Generic CCTV analytics can refer to many different systems, from basic motion rules to task-specific visual models. FrameIQ's site describes computer vision products and use cases. Compare the specific use case, supported inputs, evaluation evidence, operator workflow, integration needs, and commercial terms rather than relying on category labels.

  • What event or condition must be assessed, and how will success be measured?
  • What examples and operating conditions were used to evaluate the system?
  • Can operators review context, uncertainty, and false alerts?
  • How are cameras, retention, access, integrations, and support handled?
  • What does the quoted scope include, and what remains the customer's responsibility?

Edge and cloud deployment

Edge

Processing near the data source can reduce dependence on continuous upstream transfer, but requires compatible local compute, maintenance, and capacity planning.

Ask about device compatibility, updates, connectivity loss, operations, and lifecycle cost.

Cloud

Remote compute can centralize some operations, but depends on network connectivity, service design, data transfer, and cloud operating costs.

Ask about region, transfer, access controls, retention, availability, and recurring cost.

Questions for a fair comparison

  • Use the same representative data and agreed evaluation method.
  • Include failure cases and human review in the assessment.
  • Compare total operational requirements, not only model outputs.
  • Confirm data-handling commitments and deployment terms in writing.

Specific FrameIQ product availability, deployment options, and comparative measurements are to be confirmed. No benchmark or performance result is asserted here.
