Discussion guide

Responsible AI

Questions to discuss when planning a computer-vision or AI project. This page is not a policy, legal statement, or certification.

Principles

01

Fairness and context

Discuss where a system may be used, who may be affected, and whether imagery and evaluation scenarios represent the intended setting. Agree how errors, uncertainty, and human review should be handled. Project-specific practices are to be confirmed.

02

Privacy and purpose

Identify the purpose for image or video processing, who may access the material, what outputs are needed, and whether less data could meet the requirement. Applicable notices, permissions, and review processes must be confirmed by the responsible organization.

03

Data handling

Before sharing data, discuss collection, transfer, storage location, access, retention, deletion, reuse, and ownership. FrameIQ's project-specific controls and terms are to be confirmed; do not send sensitive data through this website form.

04

Human oversight

Decide who reviews results, what actions may follow, how concerns can be raised, and how the workflow is tested and monitored. No automated output should be treated as a guarantee of compliance or correctness.

Questions to ask

  • What is the defined purpose and operating context?
  • Which people, environments, and conditions are represented in evaluation data?
  • What is the fallback when confidence or image quality is insufficient?
  • Who can access inputs and outputs, and for how long?
  • How can people request review or raise a concern?

These points are for discussion. Approved governance, privacy, and data-handling commitments require company input.
