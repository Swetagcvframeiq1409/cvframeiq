What it does

Train on synthetic, deploy on real

AutoLabel creates photorealistic synthetic images with pixel-perfect annotations (masks, boxes, keypoints) for computer vision training. Instead of waiting for rare defects to appear on your line, you define the defect once and generate thousands of variants — different lighting, backgrounds, occlusion, sensor noise — overnight.

The pipeline uses procedural 3D assets, physics-based rendering and domain randomisation to close the sim-to-real gap. Models trained on AutoLabel data transfer to real cameras with minimal fine-tuning.

Asset creation scope, validation protocol and licensing are confirmed on enquiry.

How it works

From asset to dataset in five stages

01

Asset creation

FrameIQ builds or imports 3D assets (CAD → glTF/USDZ). Defect geometry authored procedurally or sculpted.

2-3 weeks
02

Scene graph & randomisation

Define distributions: camera poses, lighting (HDRI + analytic), backgrounds, clutter, material properties.

1 week
03

Render farm

Distributed Blender/USD render on GPU cloud. 10k-1M images per run. Pixel-perfect masks, boxes, keypoints, depth.

Hours
04

Quality gate

Automated: FID/KID vs real, domain classifier AUC, annotation integrity checks. Manual: visual spot-check.

1-2 days
05

Delivery & benchmark

Dataset in your format + training benchmark (mAP on real validation set) + fine-tune recipe.

1 week

Technical details

Specifications

Rendering engine

Engine
Blender 4.x (Cycles) / NVIDIA Omniverse (RTX) — selectable per project
Resolution
512×512 to 4096×4096 (configurable per camera)
Samples
128-512 spp (denoised), adaptive sampling
Lighting
HDRI + analytic (area, spot, sun), IES profiles, caustics optional
Materials
Principled BSDF, measured BRDF (MERL), clearcoat, subsurface
Camera
Perspective/orthographic, DOF, motion blur, rolling shutter, lens distortion (Brown-Conrady)
Throughput
~500 img/hr/GPU (A100, 1024², 256 spp)

Annotation types

Instance segmentation
COCO RLE masks (perfect, no boundary ambiguity)
Bounding boxes
XYXY / XYWH normalised, tight to mask
Keypoints
User-defined skeleton (visibility flags)
Depth / normal
EXR 16-bit float, world or camera space
Class hierarchy
Multi-level (defect → type → subtype), mapped to your taxonomy
Occlusion handling
Visible mask + full mask + occlusion ratio per instance

Delivery & formats

Formats
COCO JSON, YOLO TXT, Pascal VOC XML, TFRecord, LabelMe, CVAT
Split
Train/val/test (configurable ratios, stratified by class)
Packaging
ZIP (split), tar.gz, or direct to S3/GCS/Azure Blob
Manifest
SHA256 per file, class counts, randomisation seed log
Benchmark
Training curve (mAP vs epochs) on real val set + fine-tune config
Iteration
Active learning loop: model uncertainty → targeted re-render
