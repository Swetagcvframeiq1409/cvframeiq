What it does

Continuous compliance from existing cameras

PPE Safety Monitor connects to your existing ONVIF/RTSP cameras and runs real-time inference to detect missing or improperly worn PPE. Each camera maps to a zone with its own rule set (e.g., "helmet + vest mandatory", "gloves + goggles in chem zone"). Violations trigger instant alerts to supervisors via dashboard, mobile push, or PLC relay.

The model is trained on diverse Indian factory environments — varying lighting, backgrounds, worker densities. Inference runs on an edge appliance per 8-16 cameras. No video leaves the premises; only metadata and optional snapshot evidence.

Camera count, zone map, PPE list, alert routing and integration are confirmed on enquiry.
